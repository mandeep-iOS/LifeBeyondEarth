## Elevator Pitch (≤30 seconds)

**[0–3 sec: Show home page with planet globe]**
"Life Beyond Earth is an autonomous bio-dome simulator powered by Super Intelligence. It combines real NASA climate data, machine learning, and robot task-planning to test crop survival on other worlds."

**[3–8 sec: Click through to Planets, show Mars selected]**
"Pick a planet—Earth, Moon, Mars, or an exoplanet. Choose a crop from NASA GeneLab—Red Romaine Lettuce, Dwarf Tomato, Space Cabbage."

**[8–13 sec: Rapid montage of Mission Prep, then Simulation running]**
"Configure your mission. Then watch SI (Super Intelligence) command autonomous robot crews: the Scout picks terrain, Builders construct a pressurized dome, and the Manager grows crops inside while managing power, water, and thermal systems."

**[13–18 sec: Show Bio-Dome Lab with crisis event (solar flare)]**
"When crises hit—solar flares, dust storms, dome breaches—SI makes split-second decisions. Robots execute repairs. The SI remembers every mission, learning what crop-planet pairs survive best."

**[18–25 sec: Show Result page with survival score, telemetry, NASA OSDR citations]**
"Get a detailed mission report: survival percentage, robot actions taken, SI insights, and real NASA biological stress studies that explain why your crop succeeded or failed."

**[25–30 sec: Show Power & ECLSS Grid and Habitat Engineering tabs]**
"Dive into the engineering: autonomous power grids, water recycling, radiation shielding, and robot morphing strategies. All grounded in real NASA POWER data and exoplanet science."

**[Final frame: Logo + data credits]**
"Life Beyond Earth: Exploring humanity's agricultural future across the cosmos. Data powered by NASA POWER, Exoplanet Archive, GeneLab, DONKI, and the Canadian Space Agency."

## Key Visual Checkpoints for Video

1. **Planets page** (2 sec): Rotating globe, planet cards with irradiance values
2. **Plants page** (1 sec): Crop selection with NASA GeneLab badges
3. **Mission Prep** (2 sec): Landing site picker, robot crew selector
4. **Simulation running** (5 sec): Day-by-day timeline, health meter, temperature/power gauge
5. **Bio-Dome Lab crisis** (5 sec): Solar flare alert, robot action cards responding in real-time
6. **Result page** (7 sec): Score ring, SI telemetry summary (Memory Insight, Primary Bottleneck, Countermeasures), NASA OSDR citations
7. **Power & ECLSS Grid** (3 sec): Solar irradiance, water tank, emergency reserve kWh distribution
8. **Habitat Engineering** (3 sec): Robot mode selector, radiation shielding slider, life support balance
9. **Final frame** (2 sec): "Life Beyond Earth" title, data credits (NASA POWER, Exoplanet Archive, NASA GeneLab, DONKI, CSA Juno/LEADS), SI disclosure badge

## Technical Notes for Video Production

- **Target Resolution**: 1920×1080 (16:9) or 1080×1080 (Instagram/TikTok square)
- **Framerate**: 30 fps (web playback)
- **Audio**: Optional atmospheric sci-fi music (CC0 from FreePD or Epidemic Sound)
- **Text Overlays**: 
  - Scene titles in Montserrat Bold (white, drop-shadow)
  - Data sources at bottom-right corner (small, 10pt)
  - SI watermark on any AI-generated visuals ("SI-Generated Concept Visual")
- **Duration**: Exactly 30 seconds (per NASA Space Apps rules)
- **File Format**: MP4 H.264, ~50 Mbps bitrate, stereo AAC audio

## Talking Points (If Live Presentation)

1. **Problem**: How do we test crop survival on hostile alien worlds before sending humans?
2. **Solution**: Autonomous bio-dome simulation driven by Super Intelligence, grounded in real NASA data.
3. **Innovation**: 
   - SI learns from mission history to improve predictions
   - Robot crews adapt to crises in real-time (solar flares, dust storms, dome breaches)
   - Signal latency shapes SI autonomy levels (Moon: supervised, Mars: tactical, Deep Space: full autonomy)
4. **Data**: Every simulation validated against NASA OSDR biological stress studies—real science, not fiction.
5. **Impact**: Enables mission planning for lunar agriculture, Mars habitat engineering, and deep-space exploration.
